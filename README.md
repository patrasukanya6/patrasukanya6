## 📡 Selected research

### 🌧️ Turbulence and warm-rain formation

Using high-resolution Ka-band Doppler cloud-radar observations, I investigated how atmospheric stability, vertical motion, and **in-cloud turbulence influence the transition from cloud droplets to raindrops**.

I analysed 90 cloud-to-rain transition events using radar reflectivity, Doppler velocity, spectral width, drop size, and rain rate.

---

### ☁️ Cloud vertical structure

I have analysed millions of high-resolution cloud-radar profiles to investigate the evolution of tropical clouds from:

**shallow cumulus → cumulus congestus → deep convection**

My work includes statistical and multi-scale analysis of cloud evolution and its relationship with large-scale atmospheric circulation.

---

### 🛰️ Spaceborne radar

I have worked extensively with **CloudSat** and currently work with **ESA EarthCARE**, combining satellite radar observations with ground-based measurements for validation and atmospheric-process studies.

---

### 📊 Radar data quality

I contributed to the development of a **35-GHz cloud-radar biota-removal algorithm** designed to separate meteorological cloud echoes from biological and other non-meteorological targets.
